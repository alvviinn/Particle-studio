How it goes

Three steps, no timeline.

Bring a video

Any clip your browser can play. It never leaves your machine while you work, so big files are fine.

Pick a material

Choose gas, bubbles, polygons or grains, set the density and colours, and tell it to follow the person or object in frame.

Render and keep it

Record the whole thing to MP4 or WebM with the original sound, download it, and find it again in your library.

Built properly

Dense where it counts.

Up to two million pieces

Particles are placed and shaded on the graphics card, so you can push the density far past what a canvas drawing loop could manage.

Real depth

Brightness and subject position push particles forward and back in 3D, with slow camera drift and pointer parallax.

Follows the subject

Detect people or motion so the background thins out and the particles gather around what matters.

Palettes that match

Keep the original colours, or map the picture to Sunset, Candy, Ocean, Neon, Ember and more.

Exports with sound

Up to 1440p, recorded straight from the render with the clip’s audio. MP4 where your browser supports it.
